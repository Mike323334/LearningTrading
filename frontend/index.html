T Technical Pending
Price structure

Load market data to inspect trend and recent change.

What it means

Technical analysis studies the asset's own price and trading volume. The selected-period reference is the first available closing price in the chart period you chose; the percentage compares the latest close with that starting close. Volume is the number of shares traded during a session, which helps show how much participation supported a price move. Positive or negative describes past price evidence, not a forecast.

F Fundamental Pending
Business quality & valuation

Load SEC EDGAR facts for revenue, earnings, assets, and debt.

What it means

SEC EDGAR reports company figures from official filings. A 10-K is the company's detailed annual report; a 10-Q is its quarterly report. Revenue is the total money earned from sales before expenses. Net income is the profit left after reported operating costs, interest, taxes, and other expenses. Net margin is net income divided by revenue: 31.2% means the company kept about $31.20 as reported profit for every $100 of revenue. These are historical reported figures, not a fair-value estimate.

M Momentum Pending
Relative strength

Compare performance across the selected period and against a benchmark.

What it means

Momentum, or relative strength, describes whether an asset has been gaining or losing value faster than a comparison. Relative return equals this asset's return minus SPY's return over the analysis period, normally 3 months. SPY is an ETF designed to track the S&P 500 and is used as the broad-market benchmark. For example, +8% means the asset gained 8 percentage points more than SPY. XLK is a technology-sector ETF used as sector context, not the benchmark. This measures past relative performance, not future direction.

Benchmark: SPY | Sector context: XLK
V Volatility Pending
Range & uncertainty

Measure realized volatility, drawdown, and the stability of the current move.

What it means

Realized volatility uses the last 20 daily closing returns. Standard deviation measures how far those daily returns usually differ from their average; annualizing it makes the value easier to compare across assets. Higher volatility means wider, less predictable price swings. ATR(14) uses 14 daily sessions of high, low, and previous close to estimate a typical one-day price range as a percent of the current price. Max drawdown is the largest peak-to-trough fall in the selected chart history: the decline from a previous highest closing value to a later lowest value before a new high. A -10% drawdown means the asset fell 10% from that previous peak.

Q Quantitative Pending
Factors & correlations

Expose factor exposure and correlation so repeated evidence is not counted twice.

What it means

Correlation is the Pearson correlation between aligned daily returns, from -1 to +1. A daily return is the percentage price change from one session's close to the next. SPY is the broad S&P 500 benchmark ETF; XLK is the technology-sector ETF used for comparison. Near +1 means the asset and comparison usually move in the same direction; near -1 means opposite directions; near 0 means little linear relationship. Correlation describes co-movement, not causation.

Benchmark model: waiting for Alpaca

What it means

Macro data describes the wider economy, not this company alone. FRED supplies the federal funds rate, CPI, unemployment, and real GDP. Each value has its own reporting frequency and latest observation date, so do not compare them as if they were daily indicators.

Relative return

Asset return minus the benchmark return. Positive means it outperformed the benchmark during the selected analysis period.

Realized volatility

Annualized estimate of how widely daily returns have moved. Higher values mean more uncertainty and larger expected swings.

ATR(14)

Average true range over 14 sessions, shown as a percentage of price. It estimates a typical daily movement.

Max drawdown

Largest fall from a previous peak in the selected data. A value of -10% means the asset lost 10% from its high.

Correlation

Relationship between returns, from -1 to +1. Near +1 means they tend to move together; near 0 means little linear relationship.

Net margin

Net income divided by revenue. It shows how much of each revenue dollar remains after reported expenses.
